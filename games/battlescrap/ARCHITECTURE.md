# Current Battlescrap architecture

Standalone Vite/React/TypeScript game; no cross-game runtime imports. Read repository AGENTS.md and board-game-prototype skill before edits. Plain serializable state, seeded RNG, applyAction/dispatch and authoritative legal actions shared by UI/tests/bots. Validate before mutation; rejected actions leave state unchanged. Pending choices serialize, including combat, swaps, movement, pickup, overflow and objective claims.
Currency storage is integer half-units: display value=n/2. Budget 20 stores 40, loser 21 stores 42; hero stored cost=printed cost×2, separate from half-point heuristic estimate. Reroll stores 2, RP objective reward stores 6, RP settlement base 20 +2 per physical stone +2 victory. Do not halve AP/dice/HP/VP or round prices twice. PP and RP have distinct fields and purposes.
Content version turn-rules-v8, schema 1. Reject incompatible saves/replays clearly rather than reinterpret old costs/actions. Seed/setup/config/history replay must be deterministic. Public and bot projections omit opponent hand/objectives, unrevealed bids, RNG/seed and deck order. Owner receives off-turn death draw and private overflow control before interrupted combat resumes. Undo stops at information reveals; private exports disclose secrets only with explicit labels.
Store individual recruit action use per turn and reset at activation start. Queue/AP/D snapshots, old/new statuses, physical pickup and objective reward ledgers, settled battle IDs and stable unit/card identities must survive save/load. Settlement, objective claim and acquisitions cannot pay twice.

4. State and Privacy Contracts
Match: version, seeded RNG, scenario/config, phase/owner/activation, queue, AP/D, VP, board, units, pending/combat frames, log/history and settled battle IDs. Players: persistent roster/RP, PP/bid base/bonus/bank, selected heroes/suits/equipment/upgrades, private deck/hand/discard/objectives, physical pickup counts and public completed objectives.
Unit definition: stable ID/name/region/jobs/cost/HP/Move/Attack/range/Defense/core/two cards/portrait. Unit instance: owner, location or off-board, wounds, statuses, suit, recruit group and action-use flags. Cards have stable definition/instance IDs and exactly one zone; effects are data/handlers, no arbitrary scripts in text. Board uses axial IDs, separate raster coordinates/hit geometry, normalized wall edges and zone/control/stone data.
Keep opponent secrets out of DOM/projections/logs. Persist progress and eligibility for objectives and old/new status distinction. Never count objective rewards as secured stones. Guard every reward and settlement against replay/reload duplication.
