Preserve half-unit storage for PP/RP; dice, AP/RAP, HP and VP are native integers. Starting hand 4, limit 8; target 15 VP. Hero stats and effects come from the dated sheet snapshot. Every hero has 3–6 HP; incomplete regional entries remain unavailable.
State: serializable plain data with seeded RNG, version/schema, battle number, side, queue, captured AP/RAP, active hero, per-action hero counters, per-Recruit shared counters, element assignments, private hands and shared tactic draw/discard piles, pending responses/effects, modifier and Wind direction/usage, permanent printed-X locations, revealed traps/stones, objective eligibility/progress, ability tokens, campaign RP/wins and settlement ledger. All pending choices survive save/resume and replay.
API: newGame + applyAction/dispatch validates against legalActions before mutation; rejection leaves state unchanged. UI, bots and simulations use the same legal actions. Bots receive only their authorized player projection and public data. Hide other hands, objectives, bids, deck order, RNG and private history from DOM/projections. Pause at off-turn private overflow before resuming pending effects.
Saves: bump content/schema versions and reject incompatible old saves clearly. Undo stops at draws, dice and other information reveals. Persist battle settlement once, objective awards once, cards in exactly one zone and stable instance IDs. Save/load must not re-roll modifiers or Moonfall positions.
3. Elements and shared tactics
Fire (red flame), Water (turquoise droplet), Earth (brown rock), and Wind (sky-blue gust). Both factions use the same elements. Display element color/symbol together with faction color/insignia as a two-color identity code. For 1v1, choose one element to omit for both sides and use the same three active elements; four-hero games use all four. After roster lock, independently shuffle the active elements with state RNG and assign one to each hero on each side. Players cannot choose or swap assignments. Match map piece, queue holder and hero card.
Payments: remove fixed ability elements. A paid hero ability discards a tactic matching the assigned element, plus its escalating AP if any. Use generic rainbow ? icon. Printed tactic effects need no hero match and cannot also pay an ability. Amplification, if authored, requires its additional matching discard.
One shared tactic draw pile and one shared discard pile serve both factions; hands stay private. Each active element contributes 9 cards: two +2 Attack dice, two +2 hit damage, and one each Cancel, +2 Defense dice, counterattack-on-miss, Move {hex:4} OR heal 1, and Recruit assault. Total: 27 shared cards in 1v1, 36 with all four elements. Each player starts with 4 tactics, hand limit 8. Shuffle the shared discard into the shared draw pile only when needed; if both are empty, draw nothing. No automatic empty-hand refill. Tactic faces contain only the element and effect text, without individual card names or faction ownership. Center a large element picture in the top half; retain the existing effect layout and wording below. Keep internal stable IDs for distinct effects/copies; accessibility labels and prompts use element plus effect text.
4. Turn and combat migration
Queue: hero-only entries. Three-hero AP/RAP slots bottom→top: 1/1, 2/1, 3/1, 3/2, 4/3, 5/2. Four-hero TEST: 1/1, 2/1, 2/2, 3/1, 3/2, 4/2, 4/3, 5/2. Top and bottom are unchanged; insert intermediate steps. Lower bidder takes top and alternating slots; equal ignored bids give second faction top (TEST). All slots, including 0, are selectable; queue never draws tactics.
Actions: AP pays for active hero or friendly Recruits; RAP only Recruits, spent first. Hero Move/Attack/Grab/Respawn have independent escalating 1/2/3 counters. Each Recruit shares one counter across paid actions. Respawn does not reset it. Paid uniques have separate base-AP repeat counters; card-only repeats add no AP. Ability/tactic movement and free Recruit assault do not increment paid counters.
Checkpoints: own-turn scenario scoring and immediate victory, then Poison ticks, then hero selection. Snapshot queue entitlement before shifting to bottom. Clear selected hero’s ongoing tokens at activation start. End turn: automatic pickups/overflow; at most one eligible objective and reward; empty objective hand refill from finite deck; current authored hero end effect; fire damage; Plague spread; pass. Stop all resolution immediately on victory.
Combat: use highest face, six count only when both maxima are 6, then attacker wins ties. Hit damage = 1 + each kept attacker six + Enraged + Heavy Attack + declared tactic bonuses; Light Attack cap applies last. Winning defender gains Focus per kept six. Zero pool rolls two and keeps lower as the entire result. Snapshot and consume participating attack Focus/Enraged and defense Fortified/Dazed; new after-effect tokens survive.
Position and statuses: ordinary ranged extends to double printed range at −2 Attack dice; adjacent enemy adds −2, stacking. No ranged flanking; melee gains +1 per qualifying ally. Exact-range attacks cannot extend. Poison adds on hits; owner-turn tick deals 1 and removes 1 regardless of stack; healing restores HP and clears all Poison. Each damage transition from above 1 HP to exactly 1 gives a living hero 1 Enraged, including after healing. Recruits use HP2/Move3/Attack2/Defense2.
Terrain and keywords: use supplied 21 October map and calibrated full hexes, including nine persistent Xs. Shuffle four traps and five stones, reveal before play. Trap entry 1 damage/removal; fire entry 1 and end-turn damage. Forced movement resolves each entry and stops on defeat. Push/Pull changes distance from fixed source by exactly 1 per step, with no automatic damage/status. Fly ignores intervening units/terrain but respects destination; nothing blocks sight. Structure rules are future maps; do not invent starter structures.
5. Responses and current hero abilities
Cancel: pay card/AP/counter before opening a last-in-first-out response stack. Two passes resolve; opponent Cancel can cancel a printed tactic or paid ability, and Cancel can itself be canceled. Both cards remain discarded; canceled paid attempts retain AP/counter expenditure (TEST). No movement, buff, token or dice is exposed before the pending ability/tactic survives Cancel.
Other tactics: attacker Attack/damage bonuses and defender Defense/counter bonuses have normal pre-roll windows. Counterattack on a miss rechecks living units and range, is optional, costs no AP and cannot chain or multiply. Move/heal targets a living friendly unit on own turn; heal clears Poison. Recruit assault resolves each living Recruit’s free attack in chosen order with fresh legal targets, full response windows, overflow and immediate-victory checks.
Hero handlers: Bisti Vital Dart uses fixed ranged 3({hex:3}), Heavy Attack(2), enemy with no adjacent ally; Hester straight move up to {hex:3}, then adjacent allies +2 Fortified; Voru Pull target within {hex:4} up to {hex:3}, nearest other ally +1 Enraged per actual step; Tavi draws when a friendly Recruit ends a voluntary move adjacent to an enemy this turn; Demea token-gated adjacent Heavy Attack(2) aura; Niala ally within {hex:3} moves {hex:2}, then one adjacent enemy gains Dazed; Perrin gains 2 Enraged plus conditional adjacent ally +1 Attack; Ketu gains 2 Enraged/1 Dazed; Ilex moves another adjacent ally {hex:3}, then self {hex:3}; Orro Pushes up to two distinct adjacent enemies {hex:2}, then gives each Dazed. Sheet wording and Notes govern exact details.
Ability defaults — TEST: Voru nearest ally measured from Voru after each actual Pull step, ties chosen by acting player; zero/blocked steps grant nothing. Hester may move zero and excludes self from allies. Tavi triggers once per completed voluntary friendly-Recruit move, excludes forced movement/swaps/deployment/respawn, and expires this turn. Demea does not stack; aura checks current adjacency and ends on own activation start or defeat. Perrin’s conditional buff is an unconditional passive with a token condition, not an ability-token aura. Movement chains continue for surviving actors after another target’s hazard defeat.
6. Battlefield modifiers — TEST timing
Roll d6 once after trap/stone reveal at scenario setup after battle 1. One modifier lasts the scenario. Map and modifier RNG are stateful and replayable.
1 Plague: at end of each faction turn, snapshot all living poisoned units; each adjacent living unit gains 1 Poison if at least one source is adjacent. Both sides affected; no cascade during the same spread. Remember source for enemy Poison-defeat VP (TEST).
2 Wind: roll a fixed axial direction once per scenario. Each friendly unit may take one optional free {hex:1} step that direction during its side’s active turn. No AP/RAP or action-counter use; normal occupancy, full-hex and entry hazards apply. Usage persists through respawn in that turn.
3 Storm: ranged attacks have −1 Attack die, stacking with engagement and extended-range penalties.
4 Snow: ordinary Move allowance is reduced by {hex:1}, minimum zero. Fixed-distance ability/tactic movement, forced displacement and free Wind are unchanged (TEST).
5 Heat: when any living unit gains one or more Enraged from zero, add exactly 1 extra. Do not recurse; existing stacks gain no extra. Applies to status gains from all sources.
6 Moonfall: after random trap reveal, choose one random legal full adjacent hex per trap and add one Brightstone. Multiple stones may share a hex, including occupied/trapped/fire hexes; collect each separately. No reroll or repeat placement after save/resume.
7. Objectives, rewards and economy
Objectives: six distinct definitions in the spreadsheet Objectives tab and Design Draft; the Rules Quick Sheet contains shared objective rules only. One hidden starting objective per faction, limit 2, next-own-turn eligibility, one claim at end turn. Attack predicates record legal attacks reaching roll, whether hit or miss, while held/eligible and only on owner’s turn. Reset same-turn Recruit targeting each turn. End-position predicates use living units, permanent X coordinates and scenario spawn zones. Team Starfall requires all four heroes (TEST). Never reshuffle objectives.
Awards: objective 1 VP + 1 tactic, no RP or stone; Brightstone pickup one tactic and 2 RP once at settlement. Heroes alone drop a stone/draw on defeat; only heroes collect; Recruits cannot collect by any method. Immediate scenario victory after any VP, no later rewards/cleanup. Settlement base 10 RP and victory +1 RP remain TEST. Campaign ends at 10 wins or a 4-win lead. Catch-up RP formula and unfinished hero recruitment remain OPEN; do not invent values.
Purchases: preserve extra Recruit 3 PP; group Move upgrade 2, Attack/Defense 3 each once. Equipment: one per hero and one copy per faction, refunded on preparation deselect, retained on respawn. Boots 1 PP +{hex:1} Move up to 7; Shield 2 PP +1 melee Defense; Weapon 2 PP +1 basic Attack; Lens 2 PP +{hex:1} ordinary ranged range. Extra starting tactic 1 PP to hand limit; attacker own-die reroll 1 PP from unused bid compensation. Reject ineffective purchases.
