# Current playtest rulings
The designer authorized labeled TEST defaults for unresolved implementation details. These rulings supplement the live rules and sheet; they do not replace hero data authority.

1. **Opposed elements:** Fire/Ice, Lightning/Wind, Metal/Water, Smoke/Earth. Choose an omitted pair for 3v3; all four in 4v4. Assign distinct active faction elements to selected heroes.
2. **Mirrored decks:** nine cards per active element and faction, with two Attack and two damage bonuses and one of each other effect. Separate draw/discard piles; faction-owned upgraded card replacements preserve deck size.
3. **Upgrades:** 2 RP between scenarios; replace one basic Attack/Defense/damage +2 with +3, or Move4/heal1 with Move5/heal2. One per effect/pair, no upgrade-of-upgrade. Opposed-element slash faces are usable by either faction’s corresponding element.
4. **Team queue:** retain 1 AP/1 RAP bottom and 5 AP/2 RAP top. Insert 2/2 and 4/2 into the existing six-slot curve: 1/1,2/1,2/2,3/1,3/2,4/2,4/3,5/2. The original top RAP decrease is intentional.
5. **Team setup:** four heroes and 20 PP per faction, three free Recruits; shared faction control/hand and alternating faction turns. Normal three-hero games use 15 PP. Networked individual seats are not implied.
6. **Modifier timing:** scenario 1 has none; later setup rolls after trap reveal. Plague spreads simultaneously at faction turn end, at most one Poison per recipient and no cascade. Wind rolls one fixed axial direction; every friendly unit may take one optional free step once per own turn, after hero selection. Snow affects ordinary Move only. Heat adds exactly one extra on a gain from zero. Moonfall places once per revealed trap; multiple stones may share a hex.
7. **Hero interpretation:** the live sheet Notes record Hester’s zero-length straight move/self exclusion, Voru’s actual-step nearest-other-ally rewards and chosen ties, Tavi’s friendly voluntary completed-movement trigger/this-turn expiry, Demea’s nonstacking token-gated live aura, and Perrin’s conditional passive. Chained optional movement never spends paid-action counters.
8. **Combat timing:** roll-boundary status snapshots; new tokens survive. Light cap applies after all additions. Optional counterattacks do not chain or multiply. Cancel can cancel Cancel or a paid ability; cards/AP and the attempted paid-ability counter stay spent. Zero-pool rerolls repeat the two-dice/keep-lower procedure.
9. **Rewards and victory:** resolve each defeat once; forced-movement hazard defeats credit the enemy effect controller. Poison remembers its source, including spread. VP immediately ends battle; no post-victory overflow checkpoint is forced. Settlement pays once. Single-event attack objective predicates persist until claimed; only Break the Levy resets its distinct-Recruit progress each turn.
10. **Version/privacy:** reject v8 saves; schema 2/content v13 includes all pending frames and RNG. Projected views omit RNG, bot randomness, bids, deck order, private history and opponent faces. A played/discarded revealed tactic creates an undo barrier. Exports are labeled private.
11. **Incomplete content:** keep unfinished regional heroes and campaign offers/travel unavailable. Do not invent their stats, RP prices or uniques. Keep inherited baseline campaign income labeled TEST; no guessed catch-up formula. Bisti is an explicit portrait placeholder, not relabeled Kalawi art.
12. **Historical material:** old rules/code/tests and printed PDFs are reference history. The current Rules Quick Sheet lives in the Design Draft’s second tab and the in-app reference.

13. **Shared elements (30 October 2026):** supersedes element pairs, separate faction tactic piles and tactic upgrades in rulings 1–3. Use Fire/Water/Earth/Wind for both factions, choose one common omitted element for 1v1, and assign randomly after roster lock. Preserve 9 copies per active element, giving 27/36 cards total. Element art plus faction insignia/color identifies heroes. Tactics show element and effect only.
